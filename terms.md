## 1. Purpose

Rclone Pi5 Backup is a personal, non-commercial backup configuration for the operator's own devices and Google Drive account. It combines Rclone for cloud file transfer with Restic for encrypted backups. It is not a public backup service and does not offer accounts or storage to other users.

## 2. Authorized use

The operator must use a Google account they are authorized to access and must comply with Google's applicable terms, API requirements, and laws. Only data that the operator has the right to copy and store may be backed up. Encryption does not grant rights to store third-party copyrighted material.

## 3. Google Drive authorization

Google Drive access requires the operator's explicit OAuth authorization. The requested `drive.file` permission is used for creating, listing, updating, downloading, and deleting application-managed backup files as needed for backup and restoration. The operator can revoke access at any time in their Google Account settings.

## 4. Operator responsibilities

The operator is responsible for:

- Protecting their Google account, OAuth credentials, and Restic repository password.
- Verifying that backups complete successfully and periodically testing restoration.
- Monitoring Google Drive capacity and applicable quotas.
- Choosing appropriate backup retention and deletion policies.
- Keeping additional independent copies of important data where appropriate.
